## Data Scientist | Data Analyst

I build reproducible analytical projects that connect data quality, statistical reasoning, machine learning and business decisions.

My work combines customer analytics, predictive modeling, operational information systems and research with public data. I am especially interested in projects where the result must be both technically defensible and understandable to decision-makers.

### Technical toolkit

- **Languages and data:** Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, pandas, NumPy
- **Machine learning:** scikit-learn, classification, regression, clustering, feature engineering and model evaluation
- **Analytics:** exploratory analysis, hypothesis testing, customer segmentation, business metrics and data visualization
- **Engineering:** Flask, REST APIs, reproducible pipelines, Git, testing and data-quality validation

### Working principles

- Keep training, validation and test decisions separate.
- Make assumptions, limitations and uncertainty visible.
- Prefer clear, reproducible solutions over unnecessary complexity.
- Translate model performance into practical business consequences.
